The Digital Forging Process

From Concept to Certification

Every design passes through our six-stage refinement process before entering The Vault.

Step 01
Concept Sketching
Hand-drawn jewellery sketches are translated into precise parametric blueprints, capturing every curve, proportion, and detail before a single digital vertex is placed.
✦
◇
Step 02
CAD Modelling
Using Rhino 3D and MatrixGold, our CAD architects build the primary geometry — shank profiles, prong configurations, and stone seats — to exacting manufacturing tolerances.
Step 03
Surface Detailing
Millgrain textures, hand-engraved motifs, and micro-pavé stone placements are added in ZBrush, preserving the organic quality of master bench work at the micron level.
⬡
⊕
Step 04
Structural Audit
Our 23-point certification audit checks wall thickness, draft angles, undercut removal, and mesh integrity — guaranteeing that the file prints and casts without failure.
Step 05
Physical Print Test
Selected designs are physically printed in castable resin and cast in sterling silver. Only designs that produce a flawless casting earn our "Cast-Certified" mark.
🔬
✓
Step 06
Vault Certification
Certified designs are sealed with The Vault's digital signature and released with full commercial licensing — your assurance that every file performs exactly as specified.
