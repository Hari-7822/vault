Delete Your Account

Dimdot respects your right to privacy. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time using either of the methods below.

📱 Method 1: Delete from inside the app (Recommended)

  1. Open the Dimdot app and sign in
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation bar)
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Read the confirmation and tap Delete My Account

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out automatically.

✉️ Method 2: Email us

Send a request to:

Email: support@dimdot.in
Subject: Account Deletion Request – [your registered email]
Body: Please include your registered email address and/or phone number.

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation.

🗑️ What data is deleted?

DataAction
Name, email, phone numberPermanently deleted
Delivery addressesPermanently deleted
Subscription basket & settingsPermanently deleted
Product reviewsPermanently deleted
Order history (for accounting)Retained (anonymised)
Billing records (legal requirement)Retained (anonymised)

Retained records have your personal identifiers removed. We are legally required to keep financial transaction records for up to 7 years.
